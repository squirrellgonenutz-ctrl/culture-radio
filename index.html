TAKE THE CULTURE WITH YOU

From phone to ST.

  1. Open the live HTTPS app address in Samsung Internet or Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app, or use the browser menu: Add page to → Home screen (Samsung Internet), or Add to Home screen / Install app (Chrome). Wording varies by version.
  3. Pair your phone with the ST stereo, select Bluetooth audio, then press play while parked.

Lock-screen and Bluetooth controls are supported where your browser allows them. Samsung battery settings may interrupt background audio; if that happens, allow background activity for the browser you installed from.

This is a phone app, not an Android Auto app. Live radio needs an internet connection. Offline mode keeps the interface and favourites available, not recordings. Now Playing identifies the station; live song titles are not provided in this first version.

Station names and artwork belong to their respective owners. Culture Radio is an independent player, not affiliated with the stations.
